# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participating in this project to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards

Behavior that helps:

- Being welcoming and patient, especially with people whose first language is
  not English — this project requires English in its files, not fluency from its
  contributors.
- Accepting constructive criticism gracefully, and giving it kindly.
- Focusing on what is best for the project rather than on being right.

Behavior that does not:

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insults, derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying these standards and may edit or
reject comments, commits, code, issues, and pull requests that do not follow
them, or ban contributors for behavior they judge inappropriate.

Report abusive or otherwise unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers
through a GitHub private security advisory or by contacting a maintainer
directly. All reports will be reviewed and investigated, and the reporter's
identity will be kept confidential.
